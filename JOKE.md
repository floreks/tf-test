# Joke

I asked this repo if it had a sense of humor.

It said, "I manage Terraform and Ansible — my punchlines are all about infrastructure drift."

So I asked for a quick fix.

It replied, "Sure, give me 30 minutes and I’ll turn it into a multi-stage rollout."

And when I asked why every plan looked nervous,

it whispered, "Because in this repo, even the jokes are applied after review."
